Leadership in Context: A Comparative Analysis of Rhetoric, Key Themes, and Sentiments in Philippine Presidential Speeches

2025 • Sentiment Analysis / NLP

Performed comparative sentiment and thematic analysis on Philippine presidential speeches using natural language processing and text analytics techniques. The study used lexical sentiment scoring, topic modelling, and manual validation to surface rhetorical trends and thematic shifts across administrations.

Highlights

  • Used sentiment lexicons and transformer-based classifiers for robust sentiment estimation.
  • Applied topic modelling to discover recurring themes and rhetoric.
  • Produced visual timelines and comparative dashboards for presentation.
