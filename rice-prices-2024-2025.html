Data-Driven Insights into Philippine Rice Prices from January 2024 - May 2025: A Descriptive and Predictive Analytics Approach

2025 • Predictive Analytics / Data Science

Analyzed rice price trends in the Philippines using descriptive analytics and predictive modeling techniques to generate insights and forecasts. The project includes time-series decomposition, feature selection on market indicators, and short-term forecasting using ensemble models.

Highlights

  • Time-series preprocessing and seasonality analysis
  • Forecasting with gradient-boosted trees and ARIMA baselines
  • Actionable insights for stakeholders and policymakers
